# Flight Disruption Policies

## EU261 (EU flights)
- Delays over 3 hours: passengers may be entitled to €250–€600 compensation.
- Cancellations: full refund or re-routing at the earliest opportunity.
- Extraordinary circumstances (severe weather, ATC strikes) may exempt the airline.

## Rebooking
- Same-day rebooking offered when a delay exceeds 2 hours.
- Overnight delays require hotel and meal vouchers.
